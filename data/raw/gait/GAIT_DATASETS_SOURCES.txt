Gait-related data under data/raw/gait/

PhysioNet — Gait in Parkinson’s Disease Database (gaitpdb)
  https://physionet.org/content/gaitpdb/1.0.0/
  Format: per-trial VGRF .txt time series (not one ready-made CSV for dl_models/dataset.py).
  Project-shaped CSV: python scripts/build_handwriting_gait_from_public_sources.py
  (downloads trial files over fixed HTTPS URLs inside the script).

Kaggle — Gait in Parkinson’s Disease (tabular mirror; schema varies by upload)
  https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/zarif98sjs/gait-in-parkinsons-disease
  CLI: kaggle datasets download -d zarif98sjs/gait-in-parkinsons-disease -p data/raw/gait/kaggle_mirror --unzip
  Or: DOWNLOAD_KAGGLE_GAIT=1 bash download_gait_sources.sh

Kaggle competition — wearable freezing-of-gait (time series; not drop-in for ten summary gait columns)
  https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/tlvmc-parkinsons-freezing-of-gait-prediction
